Privacy Policy — Patrimonial Wealth

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Patrimonial Wealth ("Patrimonial", "the app") is operated by Aeternum Tech LTD ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, how it is protected, and the choices you have.

Contact for privacy matters: privacy@aeternumtech.com

1. Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Financial records you enter: assets, liabilities, investments, properties, business interests, loans, mortgages, and insurance policies, including amounts, currencies, dates, and notes.
  • Documents you upload: statements, contracts, policies, and images or PDFs you add to the document repository.
  • Information extracted from documents: when you use document-assisted entry, the app reads the document to propose fields for your review. Nothing is saved to your records until you confirm it.
  • Family and beneficiary entries: names and relationships you choose to record. Recording a relationship does not grant that person access to your account.

2. Information from connected accounts

If you connect a bank account through Plaid, we receive transaction data, account names, balances, and a cached balance with the timestamp of retrieval. We do not receive or store your bank login credentials. You can disconnect a linked account at any time.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and a device identifier used to keep your session secure.
  • Usage information: which screens and features you use, and error diagnostics. This is used to operate and improve the app.

4. How we use information

We use your information to provide and secure the app: authenticating you, displaying your consolidated net worth, importing transactions, storing and reading documents you upload, sending the reminders you configure, and answering questions through the in-app assistant using only your own records. We also use it to detect abuse, maintain service reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your financial records or document contents for advertising.

5. Sharing

Service providers that host our infrastructure, process payments, and provide bank connectivity. They act on our instructions and are bound by confidentiality obligations. The main ones are:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, file storage, and application hosting.
  • Plaid: bank account connectivity and transaction data.
  • Apple and Google: subscription billing and app distribution.

We also disclose information when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, and when necessary to protect rights and safety. If the business is transferred, information may transfer with it under this policy.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to your records requires authentication, and you may enable biometric unlock on your device. Access is limited to personnel who need it to operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active.

When you confirm deletion in the app, your account is deleted on our servers at that moment: your records, uploaded documents, connected-account links, and stored access tokens for connected providers are removed. Deletion is immediate and is not queued.

Residual copies may remain for a limited time in encrypted backups maintained by our hosting provider, and are removed when those backups expire on the provider's standard schedule. They are not used to restore your account or for any other purpose.

We retain information beyond deletion only where the law requires it or where necessary for a legal claim:

  • Records we are required to keep under tax, accounting, or anti-money-laundering obligations, for the period those laws require.
  • Information needed to investigate fraud, prevent abuse, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, for as long as that need lasts.
  • Records subject to a legal hold, court order, or regulatory request, until the matter is resolved.

We do not keep financial record contents for any other purpose once your account is deleted.

8. Your choices and rights

You can access and correct your records in the app, export your data, disconnect linked bank accounts, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information, and to object to certain processing. Requests can be made at privacy@aeternumtech.com.

9. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by email before they take effect.
